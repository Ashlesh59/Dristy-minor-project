FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you start researching with InvestIQ.

Is InvestIQ free to use?

Yes. You can create a free account with no credit card required and generate your first AI-powered company report right away.

Where does the financial data come from?

Reports are built from public company filings and market data. In this phase the platform runs on sample data while the live financial-data integration is being built.

How accurate is the AI risk score?

The score is a research aid, not financial advice. It's meant to help you prioritize where to dig deeper, not replace your own due diligence.

Can I export reports to share with clients?

Yes, every report can be exported as a client-ready PDF or printed directly from the Investment Report page.

Is my data secure?

Enterprise-grade encryption keeps every report and data source private to your organization. Full account security details will expand as backend authentication rolls out.
