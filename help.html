Frequently Asked Questions

How does InvestIQ generate its AI recommendations?

InvestIQ analyzes financial statements, market trends, and risk indicators using machine learning models trained on historical performance data, then combines these signals into a single confidence-scored recommendation.

How often is company data updated?

Financial data refreshes after each quarterly filing, while market trend and sentiment data updates continuously throughout each trading day.

Can I export a report to share with my team?

Yes. Every AI Analysis and Investment Report page includes Export and Download PDF options so you can share findings outside the platform.

What does the AI Confidence Score mean?

The confidence score reflects how strongly the underlying data supports a given recommendation. Higher scores indicate more consistent signals across financial, market, and sentiment factors.

How do I save a report for later?

Click "Save Research" on any Company Research or AI Analysis page. Saved reports appear under the Saved Reports section in your sidebar.

Is my account and research data secure?

Yes. InvestIQ uses enterprise-grade encryption in transit and at rest, and you can enable two-factor authentication from Settings → Security.

Can I change my default currency or language?

Yes. Go to Settings → Currency & Language to update your preferences at any time.

How do I cancel or downgrade my subscription?

Visit Settings → Account Settings and use the account options there, or contact our support team below and we'll help you make the change.
